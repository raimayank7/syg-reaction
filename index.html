Eight tests, no sign-up

Each test takes about a minute, works by touch or mouse, and keeps your scores only in your own browser. The four speed tests measure how fast you respond and move. The three memory tests measure how much you can hold in mind for a few seconds. The go / no-go test adds a decision to speed.

How to read your scores

A time in milliseconds looks exact, and in one sense it is: the page times your tap to the thousandth of a second. But every device adds delays of its own between the signal and your eye and between your finger and the page. So a score here is "you plus your device". It is steady on one device, which makes it good for comparing your left hand with your right, morning with night, or this week with last. It is not comparable between devices, and that is why this site has no rankings and no table of good and bad scores. Why scores differ by device explains this in full.

Games, not diagnoses

These tests are for fun, curiosity and classroom experiments. They are not medical, fitness or aptitude tests. A slow or low score on any day means nothing about your health or ability, and no score should be used to judge another person. If you have a concern about your reactions, memory, sight or hearing, please see a doctor.
