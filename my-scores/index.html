My scores

Your best and most recent results on this device, for all eight tests.

What this page shows

The table lists each of the eight tests with your best score, your most recent score and how many tries are kept. A best score for the timed tests is the lowest; for tap speed and the memory tests it is the highest. Up to thirty recent tries are kept for each test; older ones drop off.

Where the scores live

Scores are saved in your browser's own storage on this device. They are not sent to us or to anyone else, and there is no account. That has three consequences worth knowing:

  • Scores made on your phone do not appear on your laptop.
  • Clearing your browser's data, or using a private window, removes them.
  • Two people who share a device and a browser share one set of scores. For a contest, write the scores on paper.

Reading your own record

  • Best is not typical. A best score is partly luck. Your typical level is nearer the middle of your recent tries.
  • Expect early improvement. Most people improve over their first few sessions as they learn the test. After that, scores level off and wander up and down from day to day.
  • One device only. If you change phone or browser, your new scores will differ for reasons that have nothing to do with you. See why scores differ by device.
  • A slow day is normal. Tiredness, a cold room and distraction all show up.

Keeping a proper record

If you want to follow your scores over weeks or run an experiment, copy them into a notebook or spreadsheet with the date, the device and a word about how you felt. The method is on how to test fairly.

Clearing your scores

The button under the table deletes the saved scores for all tests on this device. It cannot be undone. It does not affect any other device.

These are games. The scores are not a measure of health, intelligence or ability.
